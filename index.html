THE SCIENCE

Smart-money methodology

The framework doesn't predict — it reads where institutional liquidity is taken, and trades the reaction.

Cascade-Fade

Big players run stops with liquidation cascades. The engine fades the cascade and enters the post-sweep reversal — confirmed by risk-velocity completion.

CVD & Order-Flow

Aggregated cumulative volume delta across major venues exposes real buying and selling pressure beneath the price.

Liquidity Sweeps

Down-and-back-up or up-and-back-down — the sweep direction decides the bias. Stops sit beyond the sweep extreme.

OB / ICE Walls

Order blocks and iceberg absorption mark where moves die. The framework banks profit into the opposing wall.

Risk-Velocity

Accumulation/distribution completion "dots" gate entries — quality over quantity, by design.

The Timeframe Law

Crypto majors → 1H. Indices & gold → 4H. Individual equities → Daily/Weekly. The optimal timeframe is asset-class specific, and the framework never crosses it.
