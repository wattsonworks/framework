The thesis

A week ago, the story was three semiconductors. Today the same engine holds an edge in six unrelated asset classes — including gold, the Nasdaq and long bonds.

"When it works on QQQ, GLD and TLT, it is no longer an equity strategy."

An edge that lives in one sector is usually a curve-fit. An edge that repeats across semiconductors, mega-cap technology, industrials, consumer, financials and macro ETFs is something else: evidence that the engine is reading a real, transferable phenomenon — institutional liquidity and market structure — wherever it shows up.

That is the difference between a product and a framework. The same rule-based logic — entry, structural stop, target, breakeven, time-stop — has now been validated across 1,444+ trades and six asset classes, each name run at its own timeframe and breaker setting.

It is exactly the profile a strategic partner or allocator looks for: a repeatable, explainable signal that is not confined to a single corner of the market.

Honest fit

Built for a particular kind of operator

A tech-comfortable swing / position trader — or a desk — that wants a rules engine on a focused, liquid, multi-asset book, and reads the caveats. If that's not you, we'd rather say so up front.

It's for you if you…
  • Trade a curated, liquid book across equities and ETFs at each name's own timeframe — from 5m to 4h.
  • Want rule-based entries and exits — defined stop, target, breakeven, time-stop — not discretionary calls.
  • Are comfortable applying a per-symbol recipe (timeframe, breaker on/off). This isn't one-click.
  • Understand it's a backtested edge, not a guarantee — in-sample results, real 6–19% drawdowns, often trailing buy-and-hold on raw return.
It's not for you if you…
  • Are a beginner — the per-symbol config and caveats will cost you more than they give.
  • Are a scalper — every sub-15-minute test in the data is dead.
  • Expect to "get rich," or want passive auto-money.
  • Would trade the weekly anomalies as if they were the strategy — they are not.

What this is — and what it isn't

LIQUIDEX is a disciplined, rule-based engine for a focused, liquid, multi-asset book — delivered invite-only on TradingView with alerts. The honest automation pitch is "signals you can auto-route to your broker via webhook," after you add your own position sizing and paper-test first.

These are in-sample backtests. Most trending names still trail buy-and-hold on raw return. It is not a fund-grade, audited track record and it is not passive auto-money. What it is: a non-repainting framework whose edge has now been demonstrated across six asset classes and 1,444+ trades — and that breadth, honestly presented, is the whole pitch.
