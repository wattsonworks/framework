01
The mechanism

There is a mechanism here that identifies a liquidity behaviour which recurs across different assets.

"An edge that lives in one market is a curve-fit. An edge that recurs across many is a discovery."

Institutional order flow leaves a structural footprint — resting liquidity, sweeps, the exhaustion of a cascade. Our claim is narrow and testable: that footprint is the same across instruments. The mechanism reads it, and resolves it into one rule-based trade — entry, structural stop, target, breakeven, time-stop.

If that is true, the edge should not care whether it is looking at a semiconductor, an ETF, gold, a long bond, a currency pair or a Japanese name. So far, it doesn't. The same logic has been studied across 1,444+ trades and six-plus asset classes, each at its own timeframe.

That breadth is the finding. It is the difference between a single-market product and a research framework — and it is the profile a desk, a partner or an allocator is looking for: a repeatable, explainable signal that is not confined to one corner of the market.

Research agenda

From a strong backtest to a track record

We are explicit that today's evidence is in-sample. This is the agenda that turns it into an institutional-grade result — the work a serious desk, partner or allocator expects. We would rather show the roadmap than overclaim the result.

Slippage & commission

Modelled

Every backtest is run net of commission and slippage. The figures shown are after costs, not before them.

Capacity

By design

The book is built on liquid large-caps and major ETFs — names that absorb size. Capacity is structurally favourable; the precise ceiling is not yet formally measured.

Out-of-sample

Next

Today's results are in-sample. A held-out window the engine has never seen is the immediate next test — the single most important one.

Walk-forward

Planned

Rolling re-optimisation and re-test, to show each per-symbol recipe is stable through time — not fitted to a single window.

Monte Carlo

Planned

Trade-order and returns resampling, to bound the distribution of outcomes and quantify the risk of ruin under sizing.

What this is — and what it isn't

LIQUIDEX is a disciplined, rule-based engine for a focused, liquid, multi-asset book — delivered invite-only on TradingView with alerts. The honest automation pitch is "signals you can auto-route to your broker via webhook," after you add your own position sizing and paper-test first.

These are in-sample backtests. Most trending names still trail buy-and-hold on raw return. It is not a fund-grade, audited track record and it is not passive auto-money. What it is: a non-repainting framework whose edge has now been demonstrated across six asset classes and 1,444+ trades — and that breadth, honestly presented, is the whole pitch.
