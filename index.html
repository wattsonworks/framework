THE SYSTEM

One brain. Two eyes.

Most tools hand you a dozen indicators and leave the judgment to you. LIQUIDEX is the opposite: one engine that decides, and two companions that make its reasoning visible on your chart.

LIQUIDEXThe Brain

The strategy

decides the trade

5,000+ lines of Pine v6. It reads liquidation cascades, order flow, order blocks, iceberg walls and liquidity sweeps, and resolves them into one trade — entry, structural stop, and a take-profit at the opposing wall. The risk logic and the sector intelligence are built in.

KEEPERThe Eyes · Market

Shows what's working

which name, right now

A companion indicator that runs the strategy across the equity universe and ranks every name live — by profit factor, momentum and risk. You see the leader the brain favours, not a hunch. Includes a Crypto Keeper.

PROPHETThe Eyes · Risk

go / no-go · how big

A companion indicator that makes the risk visible — a composite gauge, safe and danger zones, and a live position-size verdict that trims your size when risk is elevated and stands you down in the danger zone.

·Keeper surfaces the name. LIQUIDEX sets the trade — entry, stop, target. Prophet sizes it and breaks it in the danger zone. Keeper and Prophet don't add decisions; they let you see the one LIQUIDEX has already made.

Why semiconductors? They are the most liquid, most institutionally-traded, most momentum- and cycle-driven group in the market — and they move as a cohesive sector. Those are exactly the conditions the liquidity engine is built to read: clean sweeps, real order flow, structure that repeats.

Why that makes it credible. A system that "works on everything" is usually overfit.
