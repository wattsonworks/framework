THE SCIENCE

Smart-money methodology

The framework doesn't predict — it reads where institutional liquidity is taken, trades the reaction, and manages the risk of being wrong.

Cascade-Fade

Big players run stops with liquidation cascades. The engine fades the cascade and enters the post-sweep reversal — confirmed by Prophet's velocity read.

CVD & Order-Flow

Aggregated cumulative volume delta across major venues exposes real buying and selling pressure beneath the price.

Liquidity Sweeps

Down-and-back-up or up-and-back-down — the sweep direction decides the bias. Stops sit beyond the sweep extreme.

OB / ICE Walls & FVGs

Order blocks, iceberg absorption and fair-value gaps mark where moves die. The framework banks profit into the opposing wall.

Risk-Managed Sizing

Prophet scales position size to live risk and vetoes danger-zone entries — the difference between a good backtest and a survivable account.

Trust Tiers

Every result is graded by sample size and drawdown, not just profit factor — so you trade the robust core, not a small-sample mirage.
