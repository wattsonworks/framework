What this is — and what it isn't

LIQUIDEX is a retail product for a tech-comfortable swing trader who wants a disciplined rules engine on a focused, liquid watchlist — delivered as an invite-only TradingView strategy with alerts. The honest automation pitch is "signals you can auto-route to your broker via webhook" — after you add your own position sizing and paper-test first.

It is not a fund-grade or HFT product, and it is not passive auto-money. Selling it that way would be dishonest. It is a non-repainting, rule-based engine for the names where it has actually been validated — no more, and no less. That's the whole pitch, and it's enough.
