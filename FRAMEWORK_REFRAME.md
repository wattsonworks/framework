*Rewrites the site voice from "a multi-asset trading tool, honestly hedged" → "a systematic liquidity-research operation that profiles institutional behavior across the liquid universe." Grounded in the 2026-06 per-asset profiling findings. Citadel / Two Sigma / Jane Street register: authority comes from SCIENCE, SCALE and PROCESS — never from a return number, never from a hedge.*

## The three moves
1. **Stop selling a tool to a trader. State a research operation.** The buyer in the copy is no longer "a swing trader or a desk" — it's a counterparty evaluating an apparatus and its IP.
2. **Lead with the science that's actually new** — *behavioral profiling*. You don't just trade anomalies; you read *who* is on the other side and what they're doing. That's the differentiated, ownable thesis.
3. **Kill the smallness tells.** No "1,444 trades," no "six asset classes and up," no "+ more sectors," no front-loaded self-deprecation. Scope is stated, not counted. (One thing stays — see guardrail.)

## ⚖️ The one guardrail (this is what keeps it credible, not hype)
Fund-scale ≠ inflated claims. Citadel/Two Sigma/Jane Street project **process authority**, not "our backtest made 300%." So the rigor section **stays** — but reframed from *apology* ("these are only in-sample…") to *standard* ("the institutional bar we hold ourselves to: walk-forward, out-of-sample, Monte Carlo, capacity, live record"). Honesty rendered as **sophistication**. Drop it entirely and it reads as a hype site — the opposite of a fund.
